Team portal · 4 directions

Goal: make the portal easy to understand and easy to use for the people on the floor, and make it feel like the same brand as the website. These are frontend-only changes: same pages, same data, same permissions and the same buttons doing the same things. Each option shows the same three screens so you can compare them: the team lead queue (phone), the crew time clock (phone) and the admin home (desktop).

Todayrecorded from the live portal on 10 Oct
What every option fixes
  • Same brand as the website: navy #020A31, blue #052699, accent #9DB4FF, Benzin caps titles, Gilmer text and pill buttons.
  • Plain words: Waiting → Washing → Final check → To pay → Done. "Failed QA" becomes "Redo", "geofence" becomes "You're at Hakum Bacoor", and "Attendance" becomes "Time clock".
  • One main button per screen, named for what it does ("Start washing", "Pass · send to pay", "Clock in").
  • Filters stay out of the way until you search. No dropdowns between you and the cars.
  • Header never truncates: name, role and branch are always readable.
  • Sensible times: "longest wait 12 min" instead of "avg 1414h 43m". Numbers keep their width while loading instead of "•••".
  • Status in words and colour, never colour alone. Every tap target is at least 44px. Every icon has a label.
  • Admin menu grouped in plain words: Today · Counter · Customers · Business, with rarely used tools under "More tools".
The four optionsopen each for the full screens
Option AShowroom

Exactly the website's dark navy. Next car highlighted in blue, stages as tabs, floating dock like the customer app.

Most on-brand. Dimmer in direct sun.
Option BDaylight

Navy header and menu, light work area. The whole queue in one scroll, grouped by stage, with a button on every card.

Easiest to read outside. Closest to how the portal works today.
Option CNext step

Home is "what to do now", ranked by urgency. Every car has a 5-step progress bar, and crew see their day as numbered steps.

Easiest for new staff. Biggest change.
Option DBoard

Whiteboard columns per stage with cars as cards. Phone shows five big stage buttons plus a bottom sheet per car.

Best overview for admins. Tight on small screens.

My recommendation

B · Daylight for the phone (floor) roles, because the floor works outside in the sun and B changes the least about how people already work. Two parts of the other options would add to it:
  • C's "Your move" ranking is already at the top of B's queue. Its numbered crew steps could replace B's time clock if crew struggle.
  • D's board as an optional "Board view" toggle on the admin desktop queue, where there's room for columns.
