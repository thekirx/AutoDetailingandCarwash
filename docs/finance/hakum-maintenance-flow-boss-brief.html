Paint maintenance — full workflow

How a car enters the 6-month paint program after Ceramic or PPF, how reminders go out, how staff check the car in when it returns, and how completing maintenance resets the clock.

One schedule per plate Default every 6 months Asia/Manila dates 18 Oct 2026

3. Timeline (example)

Ceramic finished 18 Apr 2026 · default 6 months · due 18 Oct 2026.

Day 0 · Enroll

Ceramic completed. Schedule created. Status scheduled. Next due = +6 months.

Months 1–5

Quiet. Cron looks ahead 30 days only. Ops list shows overdue / due-soon / not-yet-reminded plates.

Day −30 · First auto reminder

Within 30 days of due → SMS (and push if customer account). Stamp last_notified_at. Then every 30 days until the visit.

Due window · Ops

Bookings → Maintenance: Notify client / Remind again. Or customer books from the app link.

Arrival · Start intake

TL / BA / OL / ASA / SA: creates Paint Maintenance booking at Vehicle intake, texts waiting status. Duplicate open booking → blocked (409).

Complete · Reset

Clock resets. New next due = complete date + 6 months. Loop continues.

4. Reminder rules (cron + manual)

Lead: next_due_at within 30 Manila days
Repeat: ≥ 30 days since last_notified_at
Statuses eligible: scheduled · notified
Channels: SMS and/or web push (settings)

SMS policy: outbound customer reminders only · no inbound replies · no owner daily SMS.

8. Sanity checklist

  • OK One active schedule per plate — Ceramic then PPF does not double-enroll.
  • OK Completing Paint Maintenance resets due date; completing Ceramic refreshes coated date.
  • OK Auto reminders start ~30 days before due and repeat monthly until the visit.
  • OK Arrival creates waiting intake booking and texts the customer.
  • NOTE Interval months are editable by SA/ASA (notification settings / Maintenance type row).
  • NOTE This is paint maintenance — not the detailing booking 7/3/1 day SMS reminders (those are a separate cron).
