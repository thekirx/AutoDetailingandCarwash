Process — Pay rules (pool, late, detailing, advances)

How people get paid fairly — wash pool, late/absent, detailing commission, cash advances.

Carwash salary pool

Only bay washers share. Late with a real clock earns remaining shift. Absent earns nothing.

flowchart TB
  S[Paid wash sales that day] --> P[Pool = 35% of wash sales]
  P --> R[Who was present at that branch]
  R --> W{How much of the shift?}
  W -->|On time| F[Full share]
  W -->|1 hour late on 8-hour shift| L[About 7/8 share]
  W -->|Absent| Z[No share and no car]

Detailing commission

Assigned detailer gets the detailer share. Crew share stays for bay help.

flowchart TB
  Job[Paid detailing job] --> Shirt{Free shirt?}
  Shirt -->|Yes| Net[Sales minus shirt then optional card fee]
  Shirt -->|No| Net
  Net --> Split{Detailer assigned?}
  Split -->|Yes| Crew[Crew share] & Det[Assigned detailer share]
  Split -->|No| Solo[Crew solo share]
  Det --> Branch[Stays on that branch]

Floor payroll

Official pay is confirmed on Payroll — not guessed from the close report.

As Owner or assistant with finance rights

I want to confirm floor payroll from paid sales and attendance

So that wash pool and detailing lines post once with proof

  • ✓Wash pool = wash sales × pool % × present bay washers
  • ✓Missing detailer assignee blocks confirm
  • ✓Double-pay / overlap is rejected

As Owner

I want a separate wizard for monthly salaries

So that bay floor pay and company salaries stay on separate tracks

  • ✓Fixed run = packages only
  • ✓Company packages can book under HQ when no branch is set

As Branch Admin or Team Lead

I want to see today’s compensation estimate on Crew

So that I can plan shifts without confusing estimate for posted pay

  • ✓Crew does not insert wash-pool expenses as if paid
  • ✓Banner says estimate only — confirm on Payroll

As crew

I want to see my posted payout

So that I trust My Pay over any estimate

  • ✓My Pay totals the latest posted run for me
  • ✓Owner uses Payroll instead of My Pay

Late, absent, cash advance, detailer

As Owner

I want late crew paid for the remaining shift

So that a 9:00 clock-in on an 8:00–16:00 shift earns about 7/8

  • ✓On-time present = full share
  • ✓60 minutes late on an 8-hour shift → about 7/8
  • ✓Late without a clock still uses a flat late weight
  • ✓Pesos split cleanly with no leftover centavos drifting

As Team Lead

I want absent crew off the board and off the pool

So that they get no wash share, no car, and no detailing commission

  • ✓Absent cannot be assigned a car
  • ✓Absent weight is zero
  • ✓Assigned but absent detailer holds commission until fixed

As Owner

I want approved cash advances deducted in the payroll wizard

So that advances never inflate sales and never auto-strip pay

  • ✓Only approved advances deduct
  • ✓Pending drafts are ignored
  • ✓Preview does not auto-apply cash advances

As a detailer

I want commission on every job I am assigned

So that booking pipeline and walk-in checkout pay me the same way

  • ✓Assigned person is paid on booking and walk-in
  • ✓Commission stays on that job’s branch
  • ✓Detailers are not in the wash pool

As Owner

I want each branch-day settled on its own sales

So that Bacoor wash never funds Imus coating books

  • ✓Wash pool keyed by branch and day
  • ✓Pending floor is one row per accepted branch-day
  • ✓Posted floor covers only that branch-day
  • ✓Books roll up per branch — not a merged fiction
