What it stops

The damage you
cannot polish out.

Swirls come out with a machine and a compound. These four do not — which is the entire argument for film.

Stone chips

A chip goes through the clear coat to the primer. Nothing polishes that back — it is filled or it is repainted.

UV fade

Sun oxidises pigment. Reds go pink, blacks go grey, and the damage is in the paint, not on it.

Chemical etching

Bird droppings and tree sap are acidic. Left on hot paint they etch a mark you can feel.

Lost resale

Original paint is worth money at trade-in. Once a panel is resprayed, it is disclosed forever.
