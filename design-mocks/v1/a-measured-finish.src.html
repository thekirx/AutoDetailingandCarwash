03 — Why PPF

Paint is a consumable.
Film is not.

Every kilometre takes something off your clear coat. PPF moves that wear onto a layer you can replace.

Self-healing

Minor swirls and light scratches close with heat — the film returns to flat, the paint underneath never took the hit.

Impact barrier

7.5 mil of premium-grade film absorbs the road debris that would otherwise chip bare paint permanently.

Resale

Original paint commands more at trade-in. Film is cheaper than a respray, and a respray is disclosed forever.

Replaceable

2-panel replacement on Premium and Platinum covers damaged film, no questions asked.

04 — Why ceramic coating

Wax sits on top.
Ceramic bonds in.

Traditional wax washes away in weeks. A coating chemically bonds at the molecular level and cannot be washed or wiped off.

Molecular bond

A rigid, glass-like layer that wax and sealants simply cannot replicate.

Gloss and depth

Nano-ceramic technology amplifies the paint's clarity — it reads deeper than the day you bought it.

Self-cleaning

Water, mud, and road grime bead and sheet off, so the car stays cleaner between washes.

Chemical defence

Blocks UV fade while repelling bird droppings, tree sap, and acid rain that etch bare paint.

05 — Why upgrade to nano ceramic

Not darker.
Cooler.

Ordinary tint buys privacy by blocking light. Nano ceramic rejects the heat and UV specifically — so the cabin drops without the view going with it.

Heat, not darkness

Infrared is rejected at the glass. You can run a lighter shade and still feel the difference.

Clarity kept

No mirror haze, no purple fade. Night visibility stays honest.

Signal-safe

Unlike metallic film, nano ceramic does not interfere with GPS, radio, or mobile signal.

Interior preserved

UV is what cracks your dashboard and fades your seats. Blocking it protects what you sit in.
