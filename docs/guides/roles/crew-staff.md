# Crew (staff)

**Home:** `/operations/attendance`  
**Shell:** FloorAppShell  
**Device:** Phone

## Dock

Attendance · Tasks · Forms · Advances (More: Floor)

## Daily flow

1. Time in (geofenced; a faked or tampered location is blocked and SA / ASA / BA are alerted)  
2. My Tasks  
3. Planner forms when assigned (incl. Cash advance request) · **Advances** lists your requests (waiting / approved / declined). Cash is drawer money on the Daily Sheet, not a salary expense.  
4. Time out · Attendance shows the wash pool estimate, the **typed Daily Sheet salary total** for the branch today, your current car/service when assigned, and today’s failed QA visit count. Actual personal pay still follows the approved sheet salary line.  
5. Settings → Account — save your birthday once (it locks) and update your mobile number. Attendance lists birthdays this month and banners today’s names.

## Must never see

POS, queue edit, finance, people, CRM, settings.
