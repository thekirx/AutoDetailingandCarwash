Finance & Daily Sheet computations

How every peso is calculated — from paid POS tickets, through the Daily Sheet (expenses, salaries, cash advances, drawer), to Finance P&L. Source of truth in code: src/lib/dailySheet.js, src/lib/salesSummary.js, src/lib/compensation.js, and SQL daily_sheet_server_totals.

  • Sales cannot be typed on the sheet — they come only from paid POS tickets that day.
  • Expenses and salaries on a draft do not hit the books until the sheet is approved.

2. Sales on the Daily Sheet

Only tickets with status paid or refunded count. Manila calendar date of occurred_at must match the sheet’s business date.

Gross = Σ (total + discount) for paid & refunded
Discounts = Σ discount on those tickets
Refunds = Σ total where status = refunded
Net sales = Σ total where status = paid
Transactions = count of paid tickets
Average sale = Net ÷ Transactions

Payment buckets (Cash / GCash / Card / Bank / Other) sum the paid ticket totals by method. Unknown methods go to Other — they never invent drawer cash.

Cash sales (drawer) = Σ paid totals where payment method is cash (blank method = cash)

3. Daily Sheet — expenses, salaries, profit, drawer

Every expense and salary line is treated as paid from the cash drawer. JS (computeSheetTotals) and SQL (daily_sheet_server_totals) use the same arithmetic.

Money out (costs)

Expenses = Σ amount of kind = expense
Salaries = Σ amount of kind = salary
Total costs = Expenses + Salaries
Net profit (day) = Net sales − Total costs

Cash advances (not costs)

CA given out = Σ kind = ca_release
CA paid back = Σ kind = ca_repay

Advances never enter Net profit. They only change Expected cash.

Drawer

Expected cash =
Opening float
+ Cash sales
+ CA paid back
− Expenses − Salaries − CA given out
Over / Short = Counted cash − Expected cash

If Over/Short ≠ 0, a note is required before Submit.

5. Suggested crew pay (Settings → Daily sheet rules)

Suggestions only. Branch Admin may change any amount; a change needs a reason. Pay posts when the sheet is approved.

Per person suggestion

Suggested pay = Daily rate + Wash pool share + Detailing share

Wash pool (bay crew)

  1. Wash-eligible sales = paid car wash / package lines (not merch, not PPF, not detailing).
  2. Pool = Wash-eligible sales × wash_pool_pct (default 35%).
  3. Split across clocked-in bay crew by attendance weight.
  4. Excluded from pool: Team Lead, Detailer, Admin, Sales, Marketing, Investor.
Person share = Pool × (their weight ÷ sum of weights)

Default weights: Present = 1.0 · Late = 0.7 · Absent = 0. With clock-in time, weight can be remaining shift ÷ scheduled shift.

Detailing / ceramic share

  1. Start from detailing job amount.
  2. Optional: subtract free shirt (default ₱500) and card fee % (default 3.5%).
  3. Crew % (solo default 20%, or split 10% when detailer assigned) + Detailer % (default 10%).

Team Lead / daily rate

Team Leads use staff_profiles.daily_rate_minor (only SA / ASA with finance_write may set it). That amount is the daily portion of their suggestion; they are not in the wash pool.

Monthly office salaries

Branch Admins and office staff monthly pay are entered as Finance → Bills under account 14 — not on the bay Daily Sheet.

6. Expenses & Finance P&L

When expenses hit the books

  • Sheet expense / salary lines → posted as paid expenses on Approve (once; approve again posts nothing new).
  • Finance Bills (rent, utilities, office salary, …) → P&L when status is paid / posted.
  • P&L income = paid POS only. Sheet never invents revenue.
P&L Income = Σ finance_daily_pl where kind = income
P&L Expenses = Σ finance_daily_pl where kind = expense
P&L Net = Income − Expenses
Margin % = Net ÷ Income × 100

7. Sanity checklist (does this make sense?)

  • OK Net profit ignores cash advances — advances are loans to / from crew, not shop cost.
  • OK Expected cash only adds cash sales, not GCash/card — those never entered the drawer.
  • OK POS screen and Finance sheet show the same totals (one JS module + matching SQL).
  • OK Approver cannot approve their own submitted sheet (owner / BossMich exempt).
  • NOTE Suggested pay ≠ guaranteed pay. BA override + reason is intentional.
  • NOTE Wash pool % and ceramic % live in Settings → Daily sheet rules (defaults above).

Appendix — Engineering deepening notes

Architecture · not for floor ops

Vocabulary: module, interface, depth, seam, adapter, leverage, locality.

Keep sheet totals one deep module

Strong in-process

Files: dailySheet.js · daily_sheet_server_totals · POS Daily Sheet · Finance Daily sheets

Problem: Drawer arithmetic used to leak into separate METHOD maps (BUG-081 / BUG-086).

Solution: Keep all day-money formulas behind computeSheetTotals / SQL twin; screens only display.

Wins: Locality of money bugs · One test surface · POS/Finance cannot disagree

Salary suggestion as one pay-preview module

Worth exploring in-process

Files: suggestSalaries → buildPayrollPreview → compensation.js

Problem: Understanding one day’s suggested pay requires three modules.

Solution: Deepen a single “day pay suggestion” interface that returns parts (daily / wash / detailing) without callers knowing wash-pool internals.

Server sales rollup for Finance year views

Speculative ports & adapters

Files: salesSummary.js (browser rollup over raw sales)

Problem: Large date ranges roll up in the browser (noted ceiling in code).

Solution: Optional finance_sales_summary view adapter — only if year-scale loads hurt.

Top recommendation: protect the sheet-totals module — do not re-split Expected cash math into screens.
