What changes for the team

  • One scroll shows the whole floor, grouped Your move → Waiting → Washing → Check → To pay.
  • "Your move" puts the team lead's own jobs (final check, payment hand-off) at the top, with Pass and Redo right on the card.
  • A four-number strip under the header replaces the six stat tiles.

Fixed from today's portal

  • Name, role and branch are always readable in the header.
  • Search is one field. The plate/phone history panel opens from the search results.
  • The time clock lists what's needed (at branch, location on, on time) as checks instead of a paragraph.

Scope

Frontend only: same pages, data and permissions. The team lead stays the only one who can pass Final check to payment; admins can only send cars back.
