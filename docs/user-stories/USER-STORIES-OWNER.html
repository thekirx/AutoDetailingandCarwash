The one money rule

Paid checkout tickets are the only source of day sales. The Daily Sheet is the cash-drawer and crew-pay story — it does not invent income. Finance approves the sheet once; that posts expenses and salaries. Books follow paid sales.

Same-night loop (target)

  1. Branch Admin fills and submits the Daily Sheet (POS → Daily sheet)
  2. You or your assistant approve or return it in Finance → Daily sheets
  3. Approve posts expenses and crew pay once; return sends the branch back to fix
  4. Branch Admin releases cash pay after the approve push
  5. Books / profit & loss follow paid sales — not typed fiction

Wash queue (Team Lead)

Same-day carwash only. Detailing stays on the bookings board.

I want to add and move wash tickets toward payment

So that customers flow from waiting to checkout without mixing multi-day detailing

  • ✓Queue is wash-only; detailing stays with Sales / Detailer

  • ✓Adding merch keeps the handoff; adding a walk-in service starts a new ticket

Checkout (Branch Admin)

As Branch Admin

I want to take payment for queue tickets and walk-ins

So that paid checkout is the single truth for that day’s revenue

  • ✓Cannot finish pay if a service line is missing its catalog link

  • ✓Approved cash advances show on close — not as a fake POS sales tab

Daily Sheet

  • ✓Money-in comes from paid POS (wash + detailing + merch), never cash-advance repayments
  • ✓Suggested crew pay from attendance; overrides need a short reason
  • ✓Float and counted cash close the drawer story

As Owner or assistant

Late, absent, cash advance, detailer

As Owner

I want late crew paid for the remaining shift

So that a 9:00 clock-in on an 8:00–16:00 shift earns about 7/8, not a flat penalty that ignores the clock

  • ✓On-time present = full share
  • ✓60 minutes late on an 8-hour shift → 0.875 weight
  • ✓Late without a clock still falls back to a flat late weight
  • ✓Pesos split cleanly with no leftover centavos drifting

As Team Lead

I want absent crew off the board and off the pool

So that they get no wash share, no car, and no detailing commission

  • ✓Absent cannot be assigned a car
  • ✓Absent weight is zero
  • ✓Assigned but absent detailer holds commission until fixed

As Owner

I want approved cash advances settled on the Daily Sheet

So that advances never inflate sales and never auto-strip pay

  • ✓Only approved / accepted / paid advances deduct
  • ✓Pending drafts are ignored
  • ✓Suggestions do not auto-apply cash advances

As a detailer

I want commission on every job I am assigned

So that booking pipeline and walk-in checkout pay me the same way

  • ✓Assigned person is paid on booking and walk-in
  • ✓Commission stays on that job’s branch
  • ✓Detailers are not in the wash pool

As Owner

I want each branch-day settled on its own sales

So that Bacoor wash never funds Imus coating books

  • ✓Wash pool keyed by branch and day
  • ✓One Daily Sheet per branch per day
  • ✓Approved sheet posts only that branch-day
  • ✓Books roll up per branch — not a merged fiction
