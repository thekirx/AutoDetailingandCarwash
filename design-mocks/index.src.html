Hakum Auto Care — public site, for review

Six ways to build the site

Six working prototypes of the public site. Each one is a real homepage plus one service page, using our own photography, our own fonts, and footage from our own bays — no stock, no borrowed cars. They are not six drafts of one design. They are six different answers to the question of what the site is for.

Open each in a new tab. Scroll the whole page. Try them on your phone too — all six are built for it. Then tell us which one you want built.

Set two — what the site should do

Three bets on winning the booking

Each of these picks one reason a customer leaves without booking, and builds the whole site around fixing it. Judge these on whether the problem is the real one.

All three now carry the same eight sections — hero, PPF, ceramic, tint, photos, events, branches, footer — so the only things that differ are the order they run in and how each one is treated. Each mock's own idea lives inside one of those sections rather than beside them.

What is still placeholder

  • Customer reviews in all six are written-in examples, flagged on the pages themselves. Real ones replace them before launch.
  • The origin story in B is drafted from our existing positioning, not copy anyone supplied. It needs your words.
  • Queue and slot times in F are sample figures, flagged on the page. A real build reads the live queue the system already tracks.
  • Product brand names are set in type, not logo artwork — we need the approved logo files and confirmation we are licensed to show them.
  • Whichever one wins gets rebuilt properly against the live service catalogue and booking system. These are prototypes for choosing, not the finished site.
