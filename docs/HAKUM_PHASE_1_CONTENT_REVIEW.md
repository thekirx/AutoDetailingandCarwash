# Hakum Phase 1 — FAQ claim review

Reviewed 22 October 2026 against accessible official sources. Manufacturer statements are identified as such; this is not an independent product test or legal warranty interpretation. The FAQ data contains conservative wording, without adding claims to other page sections.

## Source register

- **S1:** [ClearPro innovation](https://www.clearpro.com/commitment-to-innovation/) — coating, adhesive, and optical TPU descriptions.
- **S2:** [ClearPro PPF range](https://www.clearpro.com/paint-protection-film/) — finishes, selected gloss specifications, specialty films, and differing product warranty periods.
- **S3:** [ClearPro PPF performance](https://www.clearpro.com/clearpro-ppf-performance/) — minor-scratch self-healing and hydrophobic performance.
- **S4:** [ClearPro FAQs](https://www.clearpro.com/faqs/) — original-paint protection, removal, rock-chip protection, and broad warranty wording.
- **S5:** [ClearPro manufacturer warranty](https://www.clearpro.com/reasons-to-believe-clearpro/manufacturers-warranty/) — claim categories, exclusions, proof of purchase, and aftercare.
- **S6:** [ClearPro window tint](https://www.clearpro.com/window-tint/) — ceramic technology, heat/UV rejection, and non-metallic signal compatibility.
- **S7:** [ClearPro manufacturing capability](https://www.clearpro.com/manufacturers-capability/) — Class 1000 coating workshop and listed ISO certifications.
- **S8:** [Skin Cancer Foundation product finder](https://www.skincancer.org/recommended-products/) — named ClearPro Nano Ceramic and Magnetron Sputtering series with the Seal of Recommendation.
- **S9:** [ClearPro dealer finder](https://www.clearpro.com/find-a-dealer/) — accessible page did not establish Hakum's distributor appointment.

## Decisions for production-facing FAQ copy

| Claim | Finding | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Polyoptico™, Invisiglue™, non-yellowing optical TPU | Manufacturer descriptions accessible, S1. | Include descriptions; omit an unverified patent count/status. |
| Virtually invisible clear PPF | Supported in S2; appearance depends on finish. | Describe clear film; distinguish alternative finishes. |
| 95 GU at 60° | Listed for selected gloss films in S2, rather than every finish. | Qualify as selected gloss films. |
| UltraSatin, Cosmoblack, Carbon Fiber Black | Manufacturer range includes these, S2. | Mention range; do not imply Hakum stock availability. |
| Headlamp, windshield, skylight films | Listed in S2. | Explain availability/suitability must be checked locally. |
| Light-scratch self-healing | Supported by S3. | Include with limits for deep cuts/impact damage. |
| Hydrophobic angle 105° | S3 reports 105°; S2 includes films with other values. | Say manufacturer reports 105° for selected films, not a universal maximum. |
| Original paint and removal | S4 supports protection/removal. | Qualify removal conditions and professional assessment; preserve caution for repainted panels. |
| Warranty coverage | S5 lists seven eligible defect categories and exclusions. | Include subject to terms; retain invoice/warranty record instruction. |
| Aftercare | S5 supports three-day wait, hand washing, microfiber, and edge-pressure/automatic-wash avoidance. | Include those instructions and installer-specific advice. |
| Nano-ceramic heat rejection and signal compatibility | Supported by S6. | Include design intent; no promised temperature drop. |
| Class 1000 workshop; ISO 9001, 14001, 45001 | S7 lists them. | Attribute to manufacturer; do not claim Hakum itself holds these certifications. |
| Skin Cancer Foundation status | S8 lists HC70/55/45/35/25/15/05 NANO and HC70/25/15 PRO. | Use “Seal of Recommendation” and name the Nano series; explicitly avoid brand-wide coverage. |
| Over 99% UVA/UVB rejection | S6 states this for its window films. | Attribute to ClearPro and require confirmation for selected film; do not claim medical outcomes. |
| Day/night visibility | Varies with visible light transmission and selected shade. | Explain tradeoffs; no equal-visibility guarantee across shades. |

## CLIENT VERIFICATION REQUIRED — withheld or qualified claims

1. **Hakum is an official distributor of genuine ClearPro films in the Philippines.** S9 and the official-site searches did not establish an appointment. Provide a current manufacturer authorization identifying legal entity, territory, scope, and validity. Omitted from the new FAQ copy.
2. **All three technologies are patented.** S1 establishes named technologies, not three verified patent grants. Provide applicable patent references or explicit authorized wording. Patent language omitted.
3. **Each roll receives automated 8K computer inspection.** Accessible manufacturing/quality-control pages describe automation but did not establish the exact 8K/every-roll claim. Provide official documentation. Omitted.
4. **Distribution in over 85 countries.** Not established by the official pages reviewed. Provide a dated manufacturer source. Omitted.
5. **All Hakum ClearPro PPF has a ten-year manufacturer warranty.** S4 uses broad ten-year wording, while S2 lists different periods by film (including twelve years for some Ultra films and one year for a windshield film). Confirm the exact film SKUs and Philippine warranty terms in writing. The new FAQ promises no universal duration.
6. **All tint sold by Hakum is Foundation-certified and exceeds 99% UVA/UVB rejection.** S8 recommends specific products, and S6's general UV statement does not prove each locally supplied SKU's specification. Provide Hakum's film-to-SKU mapping and applicable data sheets. The FAQ names the recommended Nano series, attributes UV performance, and asks customers to confirm their selection.
7. **Every listed finish/specialty film is available at Hakum.** Manufacturer range is established, local availability is not. Confirm stock/orderability before promising local availability.

These client-proposed statements remain review material only. Do not promote them into customer copy without official verification or explicit client approval. No unsupported statements were added to the FAQ. Existing marketing claims elsewhere on these pages were outside the requested FAQ-only scope and were not audited or changed; reconcile any conflicting package/marketing claims before publication.
