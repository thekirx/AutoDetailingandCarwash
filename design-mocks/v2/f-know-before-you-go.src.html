Before you book

Tint questions.

The aftercare that actually matters in the first week.

Is there a legal limit on how dark I can go?
Yes. Tint shade must comply with local visibility regulations, and we will advise the legal limit for your vehicle and glass before installation.
When can I roll the windows down?
After 3 to 5 days. The film needs that time to cure fully, and rolling a window down early can lift the edge.
There is haze and small water pockets. Is that normal?
Yes. Slight haze or small water pockets during curing are normal and clear on their own within the first week.
What does the warranty cover?
Manufacturer defects — bubbling, delamination, adhesive failure, and colour change. Damage from impact or aftermarket cleaning products is not covered.
Will the price change for my car?
It may for oversized vehicles, panoramic roofs, or heavily curved glass. Existing tint removal is quoted separately. Factory-tinted glass also changes the final appearance, and we will show you what to expect.
