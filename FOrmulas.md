Reports should be aware if payment for transactions is through cash, gcash, credit or debit card and bank transfer 

Total Sales = All POS transaction 

Car wash sales = All transaction from car wash queue (Not including other transactions added by admin like refreshments, accessories etc)

Ceramic Coating Sales. = All ceramic coating sales (not including other transactions added by admin like refreshments, accessories etc)

Ceramic Tint Sales. = All ceramic tint sales (not including other transactions added by admin like refreshments, accessories etc)

Paint Protection Film (PPF) Sales. = All paint protection film sales (not including other transactions added by admin like refreshments, accessories etc)

Expenses = All manual expenses added by branch admin with option to choose expense category, mode of payment (cash or gcash)
• expenses includes salary for car wash, tinter, or detailer (when report is approve. all expenses go to finance app as expenses for the branch)
• when adding expenses, Expense category, mode of payment is required field 

Cash Advance = is deducted total money left for the daily report but is not part of expenses 

Cash Advance Payment = is added to total money left for the daily report but is not part of expenses 

Total Gcash Left = All POS gcash Transactions total + cash advance payments (gcash) - All expenses gcash transaction total 

Total Cash Left = All POS cash transaction toal + Cash advance payments (cash) - all expenses cash transaction total
