Before you book

The things
people ask.

The fine print, up front rather than buried at the bottom of a contract.

Can you put film over a repainted panel?
We do not recommend it. PPF is not recommended for repainted bodywork, and repainted areas must be disclosed before application so we can plan around them.
Will I see seams or stretch marks?
On complex curves and bumpers, minor visible seams or stretch marks are possible. Panel disassembly and edge wrapping are done only where technically feasible — we will tell you which panels those are before starting.
What exactly does the warranty cover?
Manufacturer defects: adhesive failure, bubbling, cracking, delamination, and extreme yellowing. Minor yellowing is normal aging. Film peeling caused by high-pressure washers is not covered, and neither is external road event damage.
Is the free ceramic coating the same as another layer of film?
No. The free ceramic coatings included with every PPF package are maintenance coatings — they are not additional PPF layers.
Do I need to come back after the installation?
Yes. The final installation inspection happens after 7 days, once the film has settled. It is included, and it is where we catch any lifted edge.
Can I run my coated car through an automatic wash?
Please do not. Coated vehicles should be hand washed with pH-neutral shampoo. Brush washes will shorten the life of the coating, and an annual maintenance inspection keeps the warranty active.
Will the price change when I arrive?
Pricing may change for oversized or heavily modified vehicles, and correction work is quoted after we inspect the paint. We will show you the paint and agree the number before any work starts.

Before you book

Tint questions.

The aftercare that actually matters in the first week.

Is there a legal limit on how dark I can go?
Yes. Tint shade must comply with local visibility regulations, and we will advise the legal limit for your vehicle and glass before installation.
When can I roll the windows down?
After 3 to 5 days. The film needs that time to cure fully, and rolling a window down early can lift the edge.
There is haze and small water pockets. Is that normal?
Yes. Slight haze or small water pockets during curing are normal and clear on their own within the first week.
What does the warranty cover?
Manufacturer defects — bubbling, delamination, adhesive failure, and colour change. Damage from impact or aftermarket cleaning products is not covered.
Will the price change for my car?
It may for oversized vehicles, panoramic roofs, or heavily curved glass. Existing tint removal is quoted separately. Factory-tinted glass also changes the final appearance, and we will show you what to expect.
