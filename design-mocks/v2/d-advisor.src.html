    Paint protection film

    A barrier,
    not a polish.

    Film is the only one of the three that stops a stone chip. It is a physical layer over the paint, cut to the panel and wrapped at the edge wherever the panel allows it.

    • Thickness7.5–8.5 mil
    • CoveragePartial to full body
    • Warranty5 or 8 years
    • Bay time2–5 days
    Film by ClearPro
    See the three tiers

    Ceramic coating

    Gloss that
    survives washing.

    A chemical layer bonded to the clear coat. It will not stop a chip, but it is what keeps the finish beading and makes the car far quicker to wash. We correct the paint first — we never coat over defects.

    • LayersPremium or Platinum
    • Warranty5 or 8 years
    • CorrectionAlways first
    • Bay time1–2 days
    Coating by Kisho
    Which one fits my car?

    Nano ceramic tint

    Cooler cabin,
    same view out.

    The only one of the three that works on glass. It rejects infrared at the window rather than darkening the cabin, which is why the dashboard stops cooking and the aircon stops fighting the sun.

    • RejectsInfrared and UV
    • VisibilityUnchanged at night
    • WarrantyManufacturer defect
    • Bay timeHalf a day

    Foot notes

    • 01PPF is not recommended for repainted bodywork. Repainted areas must be disclosed before application.
    • 02Complex curves and bumpers may still have minor visible seams or stretch marks.
    • 03Panel disassembly and edge wrapping are done only where technically feasible.
    • 04Warranty covers manufacturer defects: adhesive failure, bubbling, cracking, delamination, extreme yellowing. Minor yellowing is normal aging.
    • 05Film peeling caused by high-pressure washers is not covered. External road event damage is not covered.
    • 06Free ceramic coatings are maintenance coatings, not additional PPF layers.
    • 07Final installation inspection happens after 7 days. Pricing may change for oversized or heavily modified vehicles.
